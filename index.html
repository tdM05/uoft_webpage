I am an undergraduate at the University of Toronto, majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science. My research is in formal methods and AI, centered on faithfulness in autoformalization: making sure a formal proof genuinely captures the mathematics it claims to represent.

I work with Xujie Si (University of Toronto) and Ziyang Li (Johns Hopkins University) on autoformalization and faithfulness, with collaborators at the University of Pennsylvania, and with Peter Marbach (University of Toronto) on multi-agent reinforcement learning.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027. For more about me, see taddmao.com, and feel free to reach out by email.
